About Me

Designing integrated engineering systems with excitement, intentionality, and leadership.

Personal Values

Excitement

No matter what I do, excitement and passion is crucial to me. When I find excitement in my pursuits, I open the door to intrinsic motivation for exploration. This is the key to learning, growth, and building something great.

Intentionality

If you're not intentional about what you're doing, sloppy mistakes, inconsistencies, and laziness will follow. I work to be intentional, well-researched, and calculated in whatever pursuits I undertake.

Leadership

In my career thus far, I've worked to take the helm when given the chance. I feel confident in my ability to lead a team to victory, and have solid experience doing so, but also to follow another leader when they step up.
